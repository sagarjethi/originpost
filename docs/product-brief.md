# OriginPost product brief

## Promise

OriginPost turns a note, link, file, voice message, screenshot, or monitored signal into a researched and approved social post with a permanent evidence trail.

## Main experience

1. **Find** — collect direct inputs and monitored suggestions.
2. **Verify** — inspect sources, dates, claims, rights, and confidence.
3. **Create** — write platform variants and create editable media.
4. **Approve** — use a simple human review flow with clear risk controls.
5. **Publish** — schedule through official platform APIs.
6. **Prove** — store the platform ID, live URL, hashes, timestamps, approvals, API attempts, sources, and analytics snapshots.

## Initial audience

- News and media teams
- Creators and small businesses
- Agencies managing several brands
- Internal content teams

The core product has no customer-specific branding. Customer workflows are installable modules.

## Release order

1. Foundation and workspace model
2. Inbox and Content Items
3. Research and evidence
4. Writing and platform variants
5. Creative Studio
6. Approval workflow
7. Instagram connector
8. Facebook Pages connector
9. YouTube Shorts connector
10. Monitoring automation
11. Analytics and learning

## Commercial direction

The Community edition is free and self-hosted under AGPL-3.0-only. A future managed cloud may charge by workspace/brand plus metered AI and media usage. Paid-only features will be decided later; the Community edition must remain useful and distinctive.
